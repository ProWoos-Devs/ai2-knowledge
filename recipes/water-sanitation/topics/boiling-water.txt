Boiling water

Search terms: boil water, how long to boil, boiling, kill germs in water, make water safe, high altitude, mountains, fire, stove.

Written from WHO sources. Not reviewed by a water or health specialist.

For: Making water safe from germs when you have fuel

Not for: Chemicals, fuel, salt or metals in water. Boiling does not remove them. See What treatment cannot remove.

USE THIS TOPIC WHEN

- You have a pot and fuel, and water may carry germs

WHAT TO DO

1. If the water is cloudy, you can clear it first so it looks and tastes better. See Clearing cloudy water. Boiling kills germs even if it is still cloudy. [Boil, DWQ p. 142]

2. Heat the water until it reaches a rolling boil. [Boil, TN 5.2]

3. A rolling boil is enough to kill bacteria, viruses and protozoa, at any altitude. [Boil, DWQ p. 192]

4. Take it off the heat as soon as it reaches a rolling boil. Boiling for longer wastes fuel, and it can concentrate nitrate in the water. [DWQ p. 240]

5. Let it cool on its own. Do not add ice. [Boil, DWQ p. 192]

6. Protect it from getting dirty again while you store it. See Storing water safely. [Boil, TN 5.3]

7. Boiled water can taste flat. Once it has cooled, shake it hard in a closed container to improve the taste. [TN 5.2]

DO NOT

- Do not add ice to cool boiled water. [Boil]

- Do not keep it boiling longer than needed. [DWQ p. 240]

IF THINGS CHANGE

- If boiled water later gets touched by hands, cups or dirt, it is no longer safe. Boiling leaves no protection behind in the water. [Boil]

WITH NO SUPPLIES

- Boiling needs only a pot and fuel. [TN 5.2]

ALTERNATIVES USING WHAT IS AVAILABLE

- If fuel is short, use solar disinfection or chlorine on clear water instead. [TN 5.2]

IF HELP IS DELAYED OR CANNOT BE REACHED

- Fuel will run short. Keep boiling for drinking and food water, which is about 5 liters per person per day, and use other water for washing. [TN 5.1, TN 9.2]

WHAT HAS NO EFFECTIVE SUBSTITUTE

- Does not apply to this topic. Solar disinfection and chlorine are alternatives.

WHERE SOURCES DIFFER OR ARE SILENT

- WHO says a rolling boil is enough, at any altitude. The US EPA says to boil for at least 1 minute, and 3 minutes above 1,000 meters. This topic follows WHO, because fuel is likely to be short. [Boil, DWQ p. 192, EPA]

LEFT OFF THIS TOPIC

- Pasteurization below boiling point. WHO's table shows germs die below 100 °C, but none of the sources gives a household method without a thermometer.

Source: WHO Boil Water technical brief, 2015 (Boil); WHO Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality, 4th edition with the first and second addenda, 2022 (DWQ, PDF page); WHO/WEDC Technical Notes on Drinking-water, Sanitation and Hygiene in Emergencies, 2nd edition, 2013 (TN, note and page); US EPA, Emergency Disinfection of Drinking Water, updated 2026-02-24 (EPA).
