Judging food with no thermometer

Written from USDA, FoodSafety.gov and CDC sources. Not reviewed by a food safety specialist.
Last reviewed: 2026-11-27

For: Deciding about food when you cannot measure its temperature

Not for: The full list by food. See Refrigerator food: keep or throw out, and Freezer food: refreeze or throw out.

USE THIS TOPIC WHEN

- You have no appliance thermometer, or it was not in the refrigerator or freezer

WHAT TO DO

1. Count the hours from when the power went off. Without power, the refrigerator keeps food safe for up to 4 hours, a full freezer about 48 hours and a half-full one about 24 hours, if the doors stayed closed. [CDC, USDA]

2. After 4 hours without power or without a cold source like ice, throw out perishable refrigerator food: meat, fish, cut fruit and vegetables, eggs, milk and leftovers. [CDC]

3. For frozen food, the test with no thermometer is whether it "contains ice crystals and feels cold as if refrigerated". If so, it can be refrozen. [Chart]

4. Throw out any food with an unusual smell, color or texture. [CDC, USDA]

5. When in doubt, throw it out. [USDA, CDC]

DO NOT

- Do not taste food to find out if it is safe. [USDA, CDC]

- Do not trust looks and smell alone. Unsafe food can make you sick even if it looks and smells normal. [CDC]

IF THINGS CHANGE

- If the doors were opened, remember that the sources give these times only for doors kept closed. [CDC, USDA]

WITH NO SUPPLIES

- The clock is the tool. Write down when the power went off. [CDC]

ALTERNATIVES USING WHAT IS AVAILABLE

- Ice crystals in frozen food are a sign it stayed cold enough to refreeze. [CDC, Chart]

IF HELP IS DELAYED OR CANNOT BE REACHED

- With no thermometer and no way to know the hours, treat perishable food as unsafe. When in doubt, throw it out. [USDA, CDC]

WHAT HAS NO EFFECTIVE SUBSTITUTE

- A thermometer, for food in a cool box or a refrigerator with ice. The sources ask you to check the food's temperature there. [CDC]

WHERE SOURCES DIFFER OR ARE SILENT

- No source read gives a way to tell safe food from unsafe food by smell or look alone. Smell and look can only tell you to throw food out, never that it is safe. [CDC]

LEFT OFF THIS TOPIC

- Food poisoning symptoms and care. Not in these sources.
