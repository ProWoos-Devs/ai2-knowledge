About the Basic Physics Knowledge Pack

Written for AI-2. Directed to children, parents and teachers.

Where the topics come from

The topics were written for AI-2, in its own wording. They cover the physics usually taught to children from about 6 to 12 years old: forces, energy, matter, heat, light, sound, electricity and magnets. They do not follow the school program of any one country, and they were not copied from a textbook.

No teacher or physicist has reviewed them.

Simple on purpose

These topics explain each idea in the simplest way that is still true enough to build on. Later school years add detail and exceptions. For example, a topic here says that smooth surfaces slide easily, which is right for everyday things, although some very smooth surfaces grip each other strongly.

Measurements are in metric units: meters, kilograms, newtons, joules and degrees Celsius.

Trying things out

The things to try use paper, string, magnets, bowls of water, mirrors, flashlights and small 1.5 volt batteries. Nothing here asks you to use a wall socket, and you never should.

Found a mistake, or something missing?

Tell us at https://github.com/ProWoos-Devs/ai2-knowledge/issues , or change the topic yourself and open a pull request; the topics are plain text files in the folder recipes/basic-physics/topics/ of the same repository. Say which topic and what is wrong. This Knowledge Pack works offline and cannot update itself, so a correction reaches it in its next revision.
