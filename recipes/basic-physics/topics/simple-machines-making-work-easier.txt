WHAT A SIMPLE MACHINE IS

A simple machine is a mechanical device with few or no moving parts that makes physical work easier by changing the size or direction of a force. Simple machines do not magically create extra energy; they trade force for distance. By applying a smaller effort force over a longer distance, you can lift heavy loads that would otherwise be impossible to move.

THE SIX CLASSICAL SIMPLE MACHINES

1. LEVERS

A lever is a stiff bar or board that balances and rotates around a fixed turning point called a fulcrum (or pivot).
- A see-saw on a playground is a lever: pushing down on one end lifts your friend on the other end.
- A crowbar or metal spoon opening a paint lid: placing the spoon edge on the rim lets you push down with little force to pop the tight lid open with great force.
- The closer the fulcrum is to the heavy load, the easier it is to lift.

2. INCLINED PLANES (RAMPS)

An inclined plane is a flat, slanted surface connecting a lower level to a higher level. Instead of lifting a heavy 50 kg crate straight up into a delivery truck, you can slide it up a smooth ramp. You push the crate over a longer path, but the force needed to push it is much smaller than lifting it straight up.

3. WHEELS AND AXLES

A wheel and axle consists of a large round wheel fastened to a smaller central rod (the axle). When the outer wheel turns, it multiplies your turning force on the axle. Door knobs, steering wheels, screwdrivers, and bicycle pedals are all wheel-and-axle machines.

4. PULLEYS

A pulley is a grooved wheel with a rope or cable looped around it.
- A single fixed pulley (like on a tall flagpole or window blind) changes the direction of the force: you pull downward on the rope, and the flag rises upward.
- A block of multiple pulleys combined together cuts the effort needed in half, allowing construction workers and sailors to hoist heavy sails and engine blocks.

5. SCREWS AND WEDGES

- A wedge is two inclined planes joined back-to-back that splits things apart, like an axe chopping logs or a knife slicing butter.
- A screw is an inclined plane wrapped in a spiral around a central cylinder. Turning a screw drives it deep into tough timber with immense holding strength.
