WHAT THIS PACK IS

This Knowledge Pack explains the core ideas of physics for children ages 6 to 12. Physics is the branch of science that explores how things move, why things fall, what makes sounds and lights, and how forces and energy shape the universe around us. Everything is explained in clear, friendly words with everyday things you can see, touch and test at home or in class.

HOW TO SEARCH THIS PACK

Ask your question in normal, everyday words. For example, you can type "why do things fall down", "how does a rainbow form", "why do steel ships float", or "how does an electric circuit work". The search will look for the topic and passage that best answer your question.

TOPICS IN THIS PACK

Forces and Motion:
- Forces, pushes and pulls (newtons, balanced and unbalanced forces)
- Gravity and why things fall (mass, weight, and Earth's pull)
- Friction and grip (rough and smooth surfaces, brakes, and oil)
- Air and water resistance (drag, parachutes, and streamlined shapes)
- Simple machines making work easier (levers, ramps, pulleys, and wheels)
- Floating, sinking and buoyancy (upthrust, density, and floating ships)

Energy, Heat and Matter:
- Energy, movement and storage (kinetic, potential, and energy conservation)
- States of matter (solids, liquids, gases, melting, and evaporation)
- Heat, temperature and conduction (conduction, convection, radiation, and insulators)

Waves, Electricity and Magnetism:
- Light, shadows and reflection (straight lines, mirrors, and shadows)
- Color and rainbows (prisms, refraction, and why things look colored)
- Sound and vibrations (sound waves, pitch, volume, and echoes)
- Electricity and circuits (batteries, bulbs, switches, conductors, and safety)
- Magnets and magnetism (north and south poles, attraction, and compasses)

EXPERIMENTING SAFELY

The best way to understand physics is to watch it happen. You can test almost every idea here with paper, string, toy cars, magnets, water bowls and flashlights. Always handle water and heavy objects carefully, and never experiment with household mains wall sockets.
