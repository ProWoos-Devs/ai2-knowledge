WHAT THIS KNOWLEDGE PACK COVERS

This Knowledge Pack explains living things and how the human body works for children aged 6 to 12, with parents and teachers helping. Fourteen subject topics cover plants, animals, their surroundings and several body systems. Start here and About are two additional topics. The material is written for offline study and reference; it does not require another Knowledge Pack to understand an anatomy explanation.

HOW TO SEARCH

Ask a question in everyday words. For example, try "how does a seed sprout", "why is a spider not an insect", "what do lungs do", or "where does food go after the stomach". Search returns passages that may be relevant. Read their heading and content to check whether they answer your question. The first result is not guaranteed to be the right one.

READING AND DRAWING

An adult can read aloud, explain an unfamiliar word or help a child draw a model. The examples use descriptions and paper diagrams. A diagram simplifies a living system; its arrows need a meaning. In a food web an arrow follows food energy, while in a circulation drawing it can show the direction of blood flow. Different diagrams answer different questions.
