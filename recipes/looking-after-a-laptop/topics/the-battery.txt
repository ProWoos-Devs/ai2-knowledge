The battery

Written for AI-2, for the person who looks after the laptops. Not reviewed by a repair technician.

AN OLD BATTERY HOLDS LESS

A laptop battery holds less every year it is used, so an old laptop runs for a shorter time away from its charger. That is normal and is not a danger by itself. A battery that swells, smokes or hisses is different; see the topic "A battery that swells, smokes or hisses".

HOW MUCH IT STILL HOLDS

ai-2 doctor reads it. Open a terminal, type ai-2 doctor and press Enter, then look for the line that starts with Battery:

- "holds 82% of what it held when new" gives the number, and often the charge cycles it has been through.
- Under half, the line is marked WARN and adds "expect a short time away from the charger".
- "none found, so this computer works only on its charger" means the laptop has no battery, or none it can see.
- "does not say how much it still holds" means the battery keeps no such record.

HOW FULL TO KEEP IT

Makers give different advice about how full to keep a battery and when to unplug the charger, and no agency source settles it, so this Knowledge Pack gives no rule. If the laptop came with its own instructions, follow those.

A LAPTOP WITH A WORN-OUT BATTERY

A laptop whose battery holds very little usually still works on its charger. That is often the best use of it: keep it on its charger while it is being used, and unplug it at the wall when nobody is in the room, as the topic "Charging safely" says.

REPLACING A BATTERY

The U.S. Fire Administration advises that repairs to lithium-ion battery packs be done only by a certified repair facility. Never open, cut or press on a battery. Change a battery yourself only if the laptop's instructions say the user can, the old battery is not damaged, and it comes out normally without opening the case or using force. Use the replacement made for that laptop. For a damaged or stuck battery, the EPA advises asking the maker how to handle it. A battery inside the case is changed by someone who repairs laptops; do not open the laptop yourself.
