Heat, the fan and the air vents

Search terms: laptop very hot, overheating, fan loud, fan not turning, fan noise, laptop slow when hot, air vents, the AI got slower, temperature, cooling.

Written for AI-2, for the person who looks after the laptops. Not reviewed by a repair technician.

WHY A LAPTOP GETS HOT

The processor makes heat while it works, and a small fan pushes that heat out through the air vents. On an old laptop, dust in the vents and a tired fan mean the cooling often no longer keeps up. The AI-2 manual says that AI work uses the whole processor, so a laptop answering in AI-2 Chat gets hotter than one used for writing.

WHAT AI-2 DOES ABOUT IT

AI-2 reads the processor's temperature every 5 seconds. When it reaches 85 °C, or less on a computer whose own limit is lower, AI-2 lowers the top speed one step, and another step every 30 seconds while it stays that hot, never under half. When the processor has cooled 6 °C below that point, the speed goes back up the same way. The AI then answers more slowly, but the computer stays away from the point where it protects itself. On a computer whose temperature cannot be read, the guard does nothing. Most computers also have their own limit, at which they slow themselves down or switch themselves off. ai-2 doctor shows the temperature, that limit when the computer names one, and when AI-2 is running the processor slower. The topic "Checking a laptop's health with ai-2 doctor" explains its lines.

SIGNS THAT THE COOLING IS NOT KEEPING UP

- The AI gets slower after a few minutes of work.
- ai-2 doctor warns that the processor is close to its limit, or says AI-2 is running it slower.
- The laptop turns itself off while it is working hard.

WHAT TO DO

The AI-2 manual gives three things: check that the fan turns, clean the air vents, and keep the laptop on a hard surface. To check the fan without opening the laptop, give it some work and listen near the air vents, or hold a hand near them. You should hear the fan and feel warm air coming out (ordinary care, not from a source). If it stays silent and the laptop gets hot, the fan needs someone who repairs laptops. The topic "Cleaning dust, the keyboard and the screen" says how to clean the vents from outside. Let a hot laptop rest, shut down, until it is cool.

A different temperature can be set by an administrator: create the file /etc/ai2/cpu-guard.conf as administrator, write "ceiling = 80" in it (any value from 50 to 100; AI-2 never goes above 5 °C under the computer's own limit) and restart the computer. "ceiling = off" switches AI-2's guard off; the computer's own limit still protects it, but it will reach that limit more often.
