WHAT THIS KNOWLEDGE PACK IS FOR

This Knowledge Pack is for the teacher, volunteer or parent who keeps a few laptops working, often old ones that were given away, in a place with no technician and often no Internet. It says what to do every day so that the laptops last, what to do when something goes wrong, and when to stop using one.

SAFETY FIRST

A damaged battery, an unsuitable charger or an overloaded outlet can start a fire. The topics on charging, on a battery that swells or smokes, on spills and on old batteries follow the U.S. Fire Administration and the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), and each one says which advice comes from where.

WHAT IS IN IT

- Where to use and keep a laptop, and carrying and storing laptops.
- Heat, the fan and the air vents, and cleaning dust, the keyboard and the screen.
- Spills and water.
- Charging safely, the battery, a battery that swells, smokes or hisses, and old batteries and broken laptops.
- A laptop that will not turn on, and a laptop that starts but does not reach the desktop.
- Checking a laptop's health with ai-2 doctor, and a disk that is failing.
- Looking after many laptops, for a school.

HOW TO ASK

Open the Applications menu, then the AI-2 group, and choose Search Knowledge. Type the problem in your own words, for example "laptop very hot" or "battery swollen", and press Enter. Type a passage's number and press Enter to open its topic, and press q to go back to the results. If more text appears in the same window instead, type the number again to read more.

WHAT IT DOES NOT COVER

It never asks you to open a laptop or replace a part inside it. Software trouble, such as a slow computer, a full disk, updates, backups and USB sticks, is in the Linux Essentials Knowledge Pack. How to use a computer is in Using a Computer for Children.
