Where to use and keep a laptop

Search terms: where to put a laptop, laptop on a bed, laptop on a pillow, laptop in the sun, hot room, dusty room, damp room, humid, drinks near the laptop, classroom.

Written for AI-2, for the person who looks after the laptops. Not reviewed by a repair technician.

A HARD, FLAT SURFACE

Use a laptop on a table or a desk. The U.S. Fire Administration advises placing devices on solid, flat surfaces, and warns that charging on soft surfaces or blocking ventilation increases the risk of fire. A bed, a pillow, a blanket or a lap can cover the air vents, so the heat cannot get out. The AI-2 manual gives the same advice for a laptop that runs hot: keep it on a hard surface.

OUT OF THE SUN AND AWAY FROM HEAT

The U.S. Fire Administration advises not charging devices in direct sunlight. Keep laptops out of full sun from a window, and away from stoves, fires and anything else that gives off heat.

AWAY FROM DUST AND WATER

Dust that gets into the air vents makes a laptop run hotter, and the topic "Heat, the fan and the air vents" explains why that matters. Keep laptops away from sweeping, sand and cooking smoke where you can. Keep drinks and food on another table, because a spill can ruin a laptop.

A DRY, AIRY PLACE TO KEEP THEM

For e-bikes, scooters and power tools, which use the same kind of battery as a laptop, the U.S. Fire Administration advises keeping them in dry, ventilated spaces away from things that can burn and away from the building's exits. For laptops, as ordinary care, that means a dry shelf or cupboard that is not closed up tight, away from paper, cloth and fuel, and not blocking a way out of the building.
