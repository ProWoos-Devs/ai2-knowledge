A laptop that will not turn on

Search terms: laptop will not turn on, laptop does not start, nothing happens when I press the power button, dead laptop, no lights, black screen, charger light off.

Written for AI-2, for the person who looks after the laptops. Not reviewed by a repair technician.

FIRST, LOOK FOR DANGER

If the laptop or the charger smells of burning, is very hot, smokes, or the case or battery has swollen, stop here and follow the topic "A battery that swells, smokes or hisses".

STEP BY STEP

1. The charger. Check that it is pushed fully into the wall outlet and into the laptop. Many chargers and laptops have a small light that shows they are getting power.
2. The outlet. Plug in something you know works, such as a lamp, to see whether the outlet has power.
3. The cord. Look along the charger's cord for cracks, cuts or a loose plug. The U.S. Fire Administration says to replace an appliance whose cord is cracked, damaged or loose, so a damaged charger is replaced, not taped.
4. Wait. A battery that has run completely empty may need some time on the charger before the laptop will start.
5. The power button. Press it once and let go. If nothing happens, hold it down for several seconds, let go, and press it once more.

THE FAN OR LIGHTS COME ON, BUT THE SCREEN STAYS BLACK

Then the laptop is not dead. Wait a few minutes, because an old laptop can be slow to start, and see the topic "A laptop that starts but does not reach the desktop".

WHEN TO STOP

If none of this helps, the fault is probably in the charger or inside the laptop. Try another charger made for that laptop if you have one. Do not open the laptop. Write down what you tried and keep it aside, marked, for someone who repairs laptops.
