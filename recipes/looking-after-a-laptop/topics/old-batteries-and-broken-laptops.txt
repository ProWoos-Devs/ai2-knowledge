Old batteries and broken laptops

Search terms: throw away a battery, dispose of a laptop, recycle a laptop, old battery, broken laptop, dead battery, where to take batteries, e-waste.

Written for AI-2, for the person who looks after the laptops. Not reviewed by a repair technician.

NOT IN THE TRASH

The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) says that lithium-ion batteries, and devices that contain them, should not go in household garbage or recycling bins, because they can start fires. The U.S. Fire Administration says the same. The EPA adds that even used batteries can have enough energy to injure or start fires.

GETTING IT READY

The EPA advises, to prevent fires:
- putting non-conductive tape, such as electrical tape, over the battery's terminals (the metal contacts);
- putting each battery, or each device with a battery inside, in a separate plastic bag.

A battery that is inside the laptop stays inside: put the whole laptop in the bag.

WHERE TO TAKE IT

The EPA advises taking lithium-ion batteries to separate recycling or household hazardous waste collection points, sending laptops and other devices with these batteries to certified electronics recyclers, and sending batteries that come out easily to battery recyclers or shops that take them back. The U.S. Fire Administration advises asking the local authorities how used batteries are collected in your area.

A DAMAGED BATTERY

The EPA advises contacting the maker of the battery or the laptop for how to handle a damaged one. A swollen battery is damaged; see the topic "A battery that swells, smokes or hisses".

WHERE THERE IS NO COLLECTION POINT

Neither the EPA nor the U.S. Fire Administration says what to do where no collection exists, so the following is ordinary care. Until the battery can be taken away, keep it taped and bagged, in a dry place, apart from other batteries, away from anything that can burn and from the building's exits, out of the reach of children, and ask the local authorities.

THE REST OF THE LAPTOP

A laptop that no longer works may still have a good charger for another laptop of the same kind. Its other parts are inside the case and are for someone who repairs laptops. If its battery comes out without tools, take it out, tape its metal contacts and bag it as above.
