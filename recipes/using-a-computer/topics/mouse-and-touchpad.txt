Using a mouse or a touchpad

Search terms: mouse, touchpad, trackpad, pointer, arrow, cursor, click, double click, right click, left button, drag and drop, scroll, wheel, the mouse does not work, the arrow does not move, how to click.

Written for AI-2. Directed to children, parents and teachers.

THE POINTER

The small arrow on the screen is the pointer. It follows your hand. When you move the mouse to the right on the table, the pointer moves to the right on the screen. On a touchpad, slide one finger across the pad. The pointer sometimes changes shape: a hand when it is over a link, a thin line where you can type, and a small spinning sign or hourglass when the computer is busy.

HOLDING A MOUSE

Rest your hand on the mouse with your first finger (the one next to your thumb) on the left button and your middle finger on the right button. Move it by sliding it on the table, not by lifting it. If you reach the edge of the table, lift the mouse, put it back in the middle and go on.

CLICK

A click is one quick press and release of the left button. On a computer set up for a left-handed person, the two buttons are swapped. Point at something, then click to choose it, to press a button on the screen, or to put the typing line inside a box. A touchpad may have its own buttons below it. If it has none, press down on the lower part of the pad until it clicks. On many computers a light tap on the pad also clicks, if tapping is turned on.

DOUBLE-CLICK

A double-click is two clicks very quickly, without moving the mouse between them. It opens things: a file, a folder or a program picture on the desktop. If the two clicks are too slow, the computer counts them as two single clicks. Keep practicing, it gets easy.

RIGHT-CLICK

Pressing the right button once opens a small menu of things you can do with what is under the pointer, such as Copy, Rename or Delete. On a touchpad, press its right button, or press down on the lower right corner of the pad. Where tapping is turned on, a tap with two fingers at the same time does it too. To close the menu without choosing, press the Escape key or click somewhere else.

DRAG

To drag, point at something, press the left button and keep it down, move the mouse, then let go. This moves a window, moves a file into a folder, or selects several words. On a touchpad, keep the left button or the lower part of the pad pressed down with one finger, and slide another finger across the pad.

SCROLL

When a page is longer than the screen, scroll to see the rest. Turn the small wheel between the mouse buttons, or slide two fingers up or down on the touchpad.

WHEN THE POINTER DOES NOT MOVE

Check that the mouse is plugged in, or for a wireless mouse that it is switched on and has a working battery. A mouse works best on a flat, plain surface. Some laptops have a key that turns the touchpad off and on, and a grown-up can help find it.
