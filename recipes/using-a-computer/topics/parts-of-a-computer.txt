The parts of a computer

Search terms: parts of a computer, what is a laptop, screen, monitor, keyboard, touchpad, trackpad, mouse, charger, power cable, battery, ports, USB port, headphones, speakers, camera, what does each part do.

Written for AI-2. Directed to children, parents and teachers.

WHAT A COMPUTER IS

A computer is a machine that follows instructions very fast. The instructions are called programs. One program lets you write, another lets you draw, another lets you search a Knowledge Pack. You tell the computer what you want with the keyboard and the mouse, and it shows you the result on the screen.

A laptop has all its parts in one case that folds shut. A desktop computer has a box, a separate screen, a separate keyboard and a mouse, joined by cables. They work the same way.

THE SCREEN

The screen is where the computer shows you things: words, pictures and the small arrow that moves when you move the mouse. On a laptop the screen is the lid. Open it gently by holding the middle of its top edge, and do not press on the screen with your fingers or a pencil.

THE KEYBOARD

The keyboard has keys for letters, numbers and signs, and some special keys like Space, Enter and Backspace. You use it to type words and to give some commands. The topic "The keys on a keyboard" explains them.

THE TOUCHPAD AND THE MOUSE

On a laptop, the flat square below the keyboard is the touchpad. Sliding a finger on it moves the arrow on the screen. A mouse is a small object you move on the table to do the same thing. Both have buttons for clicking. The topic "Using a mouse or a touchpad" explains how.

THE CHARGER AND THE BATTERY

A laptop has a battery inside, so it can work without a cable for a while. The charger is the cable and plug that fill the battery up again from the electricity supply. A desktop computer has no battery and must stay plugged in. Only a grown-up should plug a charger into a wall socket.

THE HOLES AT THE SIDES

The small holes and slots on the sides or back are called ports. Each one takes a certain kind of plug: the charger, a mouse, a USB stick, headphones or a cable to a bigger screen. A plug only fits the port it was made for. If it does not go in easily, it is the wrong port or it is upside down, so never push hard.

OTHER PARTS

Many laptops also have speakers for sound, a small microphone, and a tiny camera above the screen. A small light next to the camera usually means it is on.
