WHAT THIS KNOWLEDGE PACK IS

This Knowledge Pack is for children aged 6 to 12 and the parents and teachers helping them. It explains ordinary health habits and what those habits can and cannot do. It works without the Internet. The writing is original, based on the CDC, NIH and HHS sources named in the About topic. It is not a diagnosis service or a source of medicine doses.

HOW TO SEARCH

Ask a question in everyday words. Try "how do I wash my hands", "how much sleep does a child need", "why do I brush my teeth", "what is a period", or "how do germs spread". Search looks for relevant passages; it can also return something that does not answer your question. Read the heading and the passage rather than assuming the first result must be right.

READING WITH AN ADULT

An adult can read aloud, explain an unfamiliar word or help a child follow a routine. Some passages are addressed specifically to parents and teachers. A child learning what a medicine or water treatment is does not mean the child is being taught to choose a dose or mix chemicals.

WHEN SUPPLIES OR HELP ARE MISSING

The topics do not assume a working tap, refrigerator, Internet connection or nearby service. Where a source supports a limited alternative, the text gives its limits. Where the sources do not supply a safe method, the text says so. A missing method is not an invitation to invent one, and another Knowledge Pack does not have to be installed to understand these topics.

SOURCES AND REVIEW
