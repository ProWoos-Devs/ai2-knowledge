About this Health and Hygiene Knowledge Pack

Search terms: about health and hygiene, sources, references, CDC, NIH, review, teacher, clinician, license, limitations, correction, report a mistake.

Written for AI-2. Directed to children, parents and teachers.

WHERE THE TEXT COMES FROM

These topics were written in original wording for children aged 6 to 12, with parents and teachers helping. Their health instructions come from the named CDC, NIH and HHS agency sources. They cover routine hygiene and prevention, not a complete medical manual. No teacher or clinician has reviewed this Knowledge Pack. An AI-assisted reading does not count as a teacher's or clinician's review.

HOW THE CITATIONS WORK

Each citation gives a short source name and the section used. "Web" means an unpaginated web page. ACTIVITY uses the printed pages of the HHS physical activity guidelines; those are real PDF page references. Sources were checked on 2026-11-10. Their titles below identify them without an Internet connection; the recipe README lists their addresses and reuse terms. The original agency information is available online without charge.

NIH SOURCE NAMES

NIH is the United States National Institutes of Health. Its institutes supply these sources:

- TEETH: National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research, Oral Hygiene.
- DECAY: The same institute, The Tooth Decay Process: How to Reverse It and Avoid a Cavity.
- EAT: National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases, Helping Your Child: Tips for Parents & Other Caregivers.
- SLEEP: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, How Much Sleep Is Enough?

HHS PRINTED SOURCE

HHS is the United States Department of Health and Human Services. ACTIVITY means its Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, second edition, 2018, printed pp. 48 and 52–53. These pages support accumulated activity time, gradual increases and activity for children with disabilities.

LIMITS AND LICENSE

This Knowledge Pack works offline and cannot correct itself. It supplies no medicine doses, chemical mixing recipes or complete treatment for an ill child. If water, materials or professional help are unavailable, a source's limits remain limits. A missing treatment is not made safe by an offline text.

The original educational writing is MIT licensed, like the AI-2 project. Agency information and public-domain facts remain under their own terms. No agency photographs, logos or source documents are reproduced. Sources and links do not imply endorsement by CDC, NIH, HHS or the United States Government. The agencies did not write this Knowledge Pack. There are no telephone numbers here or in any Knowledge Packs.

FOUND A MISTAKE OR SOMETHING MISSING?

When a connection is available, report the topic, copied sentence, problem and proposed correction at https://github.com/ProWoos-Devs/ai2-knowledge/issues or to dev@prowoos.com. For health instructions, name the supporting source and section or page. REVIEWING.md in the same repository explains the review. Corrections reach an installed Knowledge Pack only when a new revision is made available and installed.
