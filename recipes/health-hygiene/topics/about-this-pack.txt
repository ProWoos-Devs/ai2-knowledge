About this Health and Hygiene Knowledge Pack

Search terms: about health and hygiene, sources, references, CDC, NIH, review, teacher, clinician, license, limitations, correction, report a mistake.

Written for AI-2. Directed to children, parents and teachers.

WHERE THE TEXT COMES FROM

These 15 subject topics were written in original wording for children aged 6 to 12, with parents and teachers helping. Their health instructions come from the named agency sources and professional factual recommendations. The boiling instruction follows WHO's guidance. They cover routine hygiene and prevention, not a complete medical manual. A first reading, an independent second reading and a content review have taken place. No teacher or clinician has reviewed this Knowledge Pack.

HOW THE CITATIONS WORK

Each citation gives a short source name and the section used. "Web" means an unpaginated web page. ACTIVITY uses printed pages of the HHS physical activity guidelines; ASTHMA uses printed pp. 13–14 of the NHLBI school guide; BOIL uses page 1 of WHO's two-page brief. Sources were checked on 2026-11-10. Their titles below identify them without an Internet connection; the recipe README lists their addresses and reuse terms. The original sources are available online without charge.

NIH SOURCE NAMES

NIH is the United States National Institutes of Health. Its institutes supply these sources:

- TEETH: National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research, Oral Hygiene.
- DECAY: The same institute, The Tooth Decay Process: How to Reverse It and Avoid a Cavity.
- EAT: National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases, Helping Your Child: Tips for Parents & Other Caregivers.
- GASTRO: The same institute, Symptoms & Causes of Viral Gastroenteritis (“Stomach Flu”), What are the symptoms of dehydration? and Seek care right away, Infants and children; plus the linked NIDDK Dictionary definitions of rectum and pus. These are unpaginated web pages.
- SLEEP: National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, How Much Sleep Is Enough?
- ASTHMA: The same institute, Managing Asthma: A Guide for Schools, revised December 2014, printed pp. 13–14.

OTHER SOURCE NAMES

- TAMPON: United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA), The Facts on Tampons and Tampon Safety, especially Tampon Safety Tips 5 and 7.
- BRUSH: American Dental Association, Toothbrushes, updated August 2026, Key Points, Toothbrushing Methods and Toothbrush Care. Only its factual recommendations are stated in our own words; the copyrighted page and its images are not included.

HHS PRINTED SOURCE

HHS is the United States Department of Health and Human Services. ACTIVITY means its Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, second edition, 2018, printed pp. 48 and 52–53. These pages support accumulated activity time, gradual increases and activity for children with disabilities.

LIMITS AND LICENSE

BOIL means the World Health Organization (WHO) technical brief Boil water, WHO/FWC/WSH/15.02, 2015, page 1, Introduction and Conclusions. The boiling instruction follows it, in our own words; the brief itself is not included or relicensed.

This Knowledge Pack works offline and cannot correct itself. It supplies no medicine doses, chemical mixing recipes or complete treatment for an ill child. If water, materials or professional help are unavailable, a source's limits remain limits. A missing treatment is not made safe by an offline text.

The original educational writing is MIT licensed, like the AI-2 project. The source publications keep their own copyright or public-domain status. No source photographs, logos or documents are reproduced. Sources and links do not imply endorsement by CDC, NIH, HHS, FDA, WHO, the American Dental Association or the United States Government. These organizations did not write this Knowledge Pack. There are no telephone numbers here or in any Knowledge Packs.

FOUND A MISTAKE OR SOMETHING MISSING?

When a connection is available, report the topic, copied sentence, problem and proposed correction at https://github.com/ProWoos-Devs/ai2-knowledge/issues or to dev@prowoos.com. For health instructions, name the supporting source and section or page. REVIEWING.md in the same repository explains the review. Corrections reach an installed Knowledge Pack only when a new revision is made available and installed.
