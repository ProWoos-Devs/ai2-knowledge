About this Health and Hygiene Knowledge Pack

Search terms: about health and hygiene, sources, references, CDC, NIH, review, teacher, clinician, license, limitations, correction, report a mistake.

Written for AI-2. Directed to children, parents and teachers.

WHERE THE TEXT COMES FROM

These topics were written in original wording for children aged 6 to 12, with parents and teachers helping. Their health instructions come from the named CDC, NIH and HHS agency sources. WHO's boiling guidance is compared with CDC's. They cover routine hygiene and prevention, not a complete medical manual. An AI-assisted first reading and an independent AI-assisted second reading have taken place. No teacher or clinician has reviewed this Knowledge Pack.

HOW THE CITATIONS WORK

Each citation gives a short source name and the section used. "Web" means an unpaginated web page. ACTIVITY uses printed pages of the HHS physical activity guidelines; BOIL uses page 1 of WHO's two-page brief. Sources were checked on 2026-11-10. Their titles below identify them without an Internet connection; the recipe README lists their addresses and reuse terms. The original agency information is available online without charge.

HHS PRINTED SOURCE

HHS is the United States Department of Health and Human Services. ACTIVITY means its Physical Activity Guidelines for Americans, second edition, 2018, printed pp. 48 and 52–53. These pages support accumulated activity time, gradual increases and activity for children with disabilities.

LIMITS AND LICENSE

BOIL means the World Health Organization (WHO) technical brief Boil water, WHO/FWC/WSH/15.02, 2015, page 1, Scientific basis and Conclusions. It is cited only to compare the factual basis of the two agencies' boiling rules; the brief itself is not included or relicensed.

This Knowledge Pack works offline and cannot correct itself. It supplies no medicine doses, chemical mixing recipes or complete treatment for an ill child. If water, materials or professional help are unavailable, a source's limits remain limits. A missing treatment is not made safe by an offline text.

The original educational writing is MIT licensed, like the AI-2 project. Agency information and public-domain facts remain under their own terms. No agency photographs, logos or source documents are reproduced. Sources and links do not imply endorsement by CDC, NIH, HHS, WHO or the United States Government. The agencies did not write this Knowledge Pack. There are no telephone numbers here or in any Knowledge Packs.
