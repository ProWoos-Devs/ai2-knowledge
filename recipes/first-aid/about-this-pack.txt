About the First-Aid Knowledge Pack

Written from IFRC 2025. Not reviewed by a clinician.

Where the cards come from

The cards are based on the International First Aid, Resuscitation and Education Guidelines 2025 from the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC). Other health authorities were used as cross-checks. The wording on the cards is our own. IFRC and the other authorities did not write these cards and have not endorsed them.

No doctor, paramedic or first aid trainer has reviewed them. Instead, every instruction was traced to a page of the guidelines, and each card was compared against the guidelines in a separate check.

These guidelines were written for a different reader

The IFRC guidelines are written for people who design first aid programmes and for trainers, and many of their steps assume a trained first aid provider. These cards are for people without that training. We chose which steps an untrained person can safely do, so a step you may have seen in a first aid course can be missing here. If you are trained, follow your training.

The sources do not always agree, and they do not cover everything

Health authorities sometimes give different advice on the same point. When that affects a card, the card says so.

Some situations have no answer in the sources we checked. For example, we found no guidance on what to do with a tourniquet when no medical help will ever arrive. When that happens, the card says that no guidance was found. We do not fill the gap with advice of our own.

Check the date

This Knowledge Pack works offline and cannot update itself. Each card shows the edition of its sources and the date it was last checked. Guidance may have changed since then.
