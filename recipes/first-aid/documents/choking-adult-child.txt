Choking, adult or child

Search terms: choking, cannot breathe after eating, food stuck in throat, Heimlich, abdominal thrusts, back blows, my child is choking.

DRAFT, NOT FOR EMERGENCY USE

Written from IFRC 2025. Not reviewed by a clinician.

For: Adult or child. IFRC gives these one set of steps.

Age: A child is one year or older. Under one year, use the baby choking card.

Not for: Baby under one year. Use the baby choking card.

USE THIS CARD WHEN

- Something is blocking the throat and the person still responds

GET HELP

Always get emergency help if the person could not cough, speak or breathe, even if the object comes out.

WHAT TO DO

1. Ask whether they are choking. [p. 239]

2. PARTLY BLOCKED, they can speak or cry out and can breathe, and their cough is working: tell them to keep coughing and do not interfere. Think about calling for help. Stay and watch, because it can become a full blockage. [pp. 238, 240]

3. FULLY BLOCKED, they cannot speak, cough, cry or breathe, or they have only a weak cough and are struggling to breathe. They may clutch their throat, panic, or have blue lips, ears, fingers or toes. Hit firmly between the shoulder blades, up to 5 times. Adjust your force to the person's size, but it must still be strong enough to clear the airway. [pp. 238, 239, 240, 242]

4. If that fails, do up to 5 abdominal thrusts. Make a fist above the belly button and below the ribs, hold it with your other hand, and jerk sharply in and up. [p. 240]

5. If that fails, get emergency help immediately. [p. 240]

6. Keep alternating 5 blows and 5 thrusts until the blockage clears or the person stops responding. [p. 240]

DO NOT

- Do not sweep the mouth blindly with your fingers. It can push the object further down. Remove something only if you can see it. [pp. 239, 240]

- Do not routinely use a suction device. [p. 239]

- Do not give a drink to clear the blockage. [p. 242]

IF THINGS CHANGE

- If the person stops responding, start CPR using the card for their age. Give breaths as well as compressions if you possibly can, as these may dislodge the object. [pp. 238, 240, 242]

WITH NO MEDICAL SUPPLIES

- Every step on this card is done with your hands. No equipment is needed. [p. 240]

ALTERNATIVES USING WHAT IS AVAILABLE

None known in the sources used for this card.

IF HELP IS DELAYED OR CANNOT BE REACHED

- Do not stop. Keep alternating blows and thrusts. [p. 240]

- After a full blockage clears, medical care is still needed, because there may be internal injury, airway damage or a piece left in the lungs. No guidance was found in the sources checked on what to do if care cannot be reached. [p. 241]

- After a partial blockage, the person should get medical care if they keep coughing, have trouble swallowing, or feel the object is still stuck. [p. 241]

WHAT HAS NO EFFECTIVE SUBSTITUTE

- IFRC's steps name no other way to clear a full blockage when these actions fail, apart from CPR once the person stops responding. [pp. 240, 241]

WHERE SOURCES DIFFER OR ARE SILENT

- Gap: this card has no adapted thrust for a pregnant or obese person, because IFRC's main text does not give one.

- IFRC is not consistent on a weak cough. Its guidelines treat a weakened or ineffective cough as a complete blockage needing back blows (p. 238), while its list of signs puts weak coughing under partial blockage (p. 239). This card follows the guideline, so a weak cough gets back blows.

- Chest thrusts: IFRC's good practice points say they work as well as back blows or abdominal thrusts in a responsive adult or child (p. 238). Its evidence review supports starting with back blows and using chest thrusts mainly in unconscious people (pp. 243-244), and its steps do not describe them. This card follows the steps and does not teach them.

LEFT OFF THIS CARD

- Thrust adaptation for pregnant or obese people. IFRC refers to a separate training resource that was not read. No guidance is given here for these people beyond the steps above.

Source: IFRC International First Aid, Resuscitation and Education Guidelines 2025. Page numbers in square brackets are pages of that source.
