Subtraction, taking away and finding difference

Written for AI-2. Directed to children, parents and teachers.

WHAT SUBTRACTION MEANS

Subtraction means taking one amount away from another or finding the difference between two numbers. The symbol for subtraction is the minus sign (-). The starting number is called the minuend, the amount taken away is the subtrahend, and the answer is called the difference. In 9 - 4 = 5, 5 is the difference.

TWO WAYS TO THINK ABOUT SUBTRACTION

Subtraction answers two common questions:
1. "Taking away": You have 8 apples and eat 3. How many are left? 8 - 3 = 5.
2. "Comparing / finding the gap": Anna has 9 pencils and Leo has 4. How many more pencils does Anna have? The difference between 9 and 4 is 5.

ORDER MATTERS IN SUBTRACTION

Unlike addition, the order of numbers in subtraction cannot be swapped. 9 - 4 equals 5, but 4 - 9 does not equal 5. It goes below zero and equals -5, which the topic on negative numbers explains. In most first subtraction problems the bigger number comes first.

CHECKING SUBTRACTION WITH ADDITION

Addition and subtraction are inverse operations, which means one undoes the other. You can always check your subtraction answer by adding the difference back to the amount you took away. If you calculate 14 - 6 = 8, check it by calculating 8 + 6. Since 8 + 6 = 14, your subtraction is correct.

COLUMN SUBTRACTION AND BORROWING (REGROUPING)

When subtracting larger numbers, write them vertically in place value columns and start from the ones on the right:
1. Look at the top digit in the ones column. If it is equal to or larger than the bottom digit, subtract normally.
2. If the top digit is smaller than the bottom digit, you cannot take away directly without borrowing.
3. Borrow (regroup) 1 ten from the tens column to the left. Cross out the tens digit and write one less above it.
4. Give that 1 ten (which equals 10 ones) to your ones column, adding 10 to the top ones digit.
5. Subtract the ones column now that the top number is larger.
6. Move left to the tens column and subtract the remaining tens.
For example, in 52 - 18:
In the ones column, 2 is smaller than 8. Borrow 1 ten from the 5 tens (leaving 4 tens). The 2 becomes 12.
Now subtract ones: 12 - 8 = 4.
Subtract tens: 4 - 1 = 3. The answer is 34.

WHEN THE NEXT COLUMN HAS A ZERO

Sometimes there are no tens to regroup, as in 402 - 178. Look farther left for a digit that is not zero. Regroup 1 hundred into 10 tens, giving 3 hundreds, 10 tens and 2 ones. Then regroup 1 of those tens into 10 ones, giving 3 hundreds, 9 tens and 12 ones.
Now subtract each column: 12 - 8 = 4 ones, 9 - 7 = 2 tens, 3 - 1 = 2 hundreds. The answer is 224.
