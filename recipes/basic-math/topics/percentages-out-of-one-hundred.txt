Percentages and parts out of one hundred

Search terms: percentages, percent, percent sign, parts per hundred, out of 100, finding fifty percent, finding ten percent, discounts.

Written for AI-2. Directed to children, parents and teachers.

WHAT PERCENT MEANS

Percent means parts out of one hundred. The word comes from the Latin "per centum", which means "by the hundred". The symbol for percent is %. A percentage is simply a fraction with a denominator of 100, where 50% means 50 out of 100, and 100% means the entire whole thing.

EVERYDAY EXAMPLES OF PERCENTAGES

- Battery level: A tablet or phone showing 100% has a completely full battery. At 50%, half the battery charge has been used. At 10%, only a small tenth of charge is left.
- Test scores: Getting 80% on a test means you got 80 points right out of every 100 points.
- Weather: A 0% chance of rain means no rain is expected; a 100% chance means rain is certain.

KEY PERCENTAGES TO REMEMBER

- 100% is the whole amount (100/100 = 1).
- 50% is half of the amount (50/100 = 1/2 = 0.5). To find 50% of any number, divide it by 2. For example, 50% of 20 is 10.
- 25% is one-quarter of the amount (25/100 = 1/4 = 0.25). To find 25% of any number, halve it and halve it again (divide by 4). 25% of 20 is 5.
- 75% is three-quarters of the amount (75/100 = 3/4 = 0.75).
- 10% is one-tenth of the amount (10/100 = 1/10 = 0.1). To find 10% of any number, simply divide it by 10. For example, 10% of 80 is 8.
- 1% is one-hundredth of the amount (1/100 = 0.01). To find 1% of a number, divide it by 100.

HOW TO FIND A PERCENTAGE OF A NUMBER

To find a percentage quickly in your head, use the 10% and 50% benchmarks as stepping stones:
- To find 20% of 60: Find 10% first (60 ÷ 10 = 6), then double it (6 x 2 = 12).
- To find 5% of 60: Find 10% first (6), then take half of that (3).
- To find 15% of 60: Add 10% (6) and 5% (3) together to get 9.
Building percentages from 10% and 1% allows you to solve almost any everyday percentage question without complex formulas.
