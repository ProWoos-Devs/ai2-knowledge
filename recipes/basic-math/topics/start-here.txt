WHAT THIS PACK IS

This Knowledge Pack explains the building blocks of mathematics for children ages 6 to 12. Mathematics is the science of numbers, patterns, shapes and measurements that help us understand and solve problems in everyday life. Everything in this pack is written in clear, simple language with real-world examples you can picture or try with everyday objects.

HOW TO SEARCH THIS PACK

Ask your question in normal everyday words. For example, you can type "what is a numerator", "what is place value", "difference between perimeter and area", or "how to read an analog clock". The search will bring you the topic that best matches your question.

TOPICS IN THIS PACK

Numbers and Arithmetic:
- Counting, digits and place value (how numbers are built with ones, tens and hundreds)
- Addition and combining numbers (putting numbers together and carrying over)
- Subtraction, taking away and finding difference (borrowing and checking)
- Multiplication, equal groups and times tables (arrays and patterns)
- Division, sharing equally and remainders (sharing into equal piles)

Fractions and Decimals:
- Fractions and parts of a whole (halves, thirds, quarters, numerators and denominators)
- Decimals, tenths, hundredths and money (numbers with decimal points)
- Percentages and parts out of one hundred (finding 50%, 25% and 10%)
- Negative numbers and counting below zero (temperatures and number lines)

Shapes and Measurement:
- Shapes in 2D and 3D geometry (sides, corners, faces and edges)
- Perimeter and area of flat shapes (measuring boundaries and flat space inside)
- Measuring length, mass, weight and volume (meters, kilograms, liters and metric prefixes)
- Telling time, clocks and the calendar (reading clock hands, hours, minutes and dates)

TIPS FOR LEARNING MATH

Take your time and picture what the numbers mean. Math is not about memorizing magic tricks; it is about seeing how things connect. If an idea feels tricky, draw it with dots on paper, count with small pebbles or beans, or talk through the steps out loud.
