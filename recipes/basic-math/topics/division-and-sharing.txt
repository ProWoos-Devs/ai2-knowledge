Division, sharing equally and remainders

Search terms: division, dividing, share equally, divided by, quotient, remainder, left over, grouping, how many times does it fit, divide, sharing, split, divide by zero.

Written for AI-2. Directed to children, parents and teachers.

WHAT DIVISION MEANS

Division means splitting a whole amount into equal groups or sharing it fairly among friends. The symbol for division is the divide sign (÷) or a forward slash (/). The number you start with is called the dividend, the number of groups you split it into is the divisor, and the answer is called the quotient. For example, in 12 ÷ 3 = 4, 12 is shared into 3 equal piles of 4.

TWO WAYS TO THINK ABOUT DIVISION

1. "Sharing equally": You have 15 cookies to share fairly among 3 children. Handing them out one by one gives each child 5 cookies (15 ÷ 3 = 5).
2. "Grouping / fitting into": You have 15 cookies and each party bag takes 3 cookies. How many full bags can you make? Count groups of 3 until you reach 15 (3, 6, 9, 12, 15), which gives 5 bags.

DIVISION UNDOES MULTIPLICATION

Multiplication and division belong to the same fact family. If you know that 4 x 5 = 20, you automatically know two division facts:
- 20 ÷ 4 = 5
- 20 ÷ 5 = 4
If you get stuck on a division problem like 24 ÷ 6, ask yourself: "What number multiplied by 6 gives 24?" The answer is 4.

WHAT TO DO WITH REMAINDERS

A remainder is the amount left over when a number cannot be divided into completely equal whole parts.
For example, if you share 14 stickers equally among 3 children:
- Each child receives 4 stickers (since 3 x 4 = 12).
- There are 2 stickers left over that cannot be shared without cutting them.
We write this as: 14 ÷ 3 = 4 remainder 2 (or 4 R2).
The remainder must always be smaller than the divisor. If your remainder is equal to or larger than the number you divided by, you could have given each group another item.

DIVIDING BY 1 AND DIVIDING BY 0

Dividing any number by 1 gives the original number itself (8 ÷ 1 = 8).
Dividing zero by any number except zero gives zero (0 ÷ 4 = 0).
You cannot divide any number by zero in mathematics; it is impossible to share objects into zero groups.
