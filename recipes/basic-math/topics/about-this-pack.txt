About the Basic Math Knowledge Pack

Written for AI-2. Directed to children, parents and teachers.

Where the topics come from

The topics were written for AI-2, in its own wording. They cover the arithmetic, fractions, decimals, shapes and measurement usually taught to children from about 6 to 12 years old. They do not follow the school program of any one country, and they were not copied from a textbook.

No teacher has reviewed them.

How numbers and words are written here

Measurements are in metric units: meters, grams and liters.

Decimals are written with a point, as in 2.75, and large numbers with a comma between groups of three digits, as in 1,000. Many countries do it the other way around and write 2,75 and 1.000. The amounts are the same; only the marks differ. Use the way your school uses.

Some words also change from place to place. Math is called maths in many countries, and carrying and borrowing are both also called regrouping.

Found a mistake, or something missing?
